Two-tier market

What the coverage describes

Serious collectors pay for authentic imports. Budget-conscious fans are reported to split between authentic and counterfeit goods, and one Kolkata retailer says collectible sales have fallen partly because of fakes.

What is not known

Gaps to fill with MIRAI data

No source found sizes the counterfeit market in India. The survey did not measure price sensitivity either, so the link between price and lower collectible purchases remains a hypothesis.
